Privacy Policy — My Zombie World

This policy applies to the mobile game My Zombie World (App Store name: Pixels Horror Zombie World, "the Game"). The Game is a single-player game and stores your progress locally on your device. The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, and the ad SDK may collect and use limited data as described below.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. Information we collect

The Game does not require an account and does not upload your game saves. Ad-related data is collected by the ad SDK:

  • Device identifiers: advertising identifier (IDFA, only when you allow tracking), identifier for vendor (IDFV), IP address;
  • Advertising data: ad impressions, clicks, and approximate location inferred from IP address;
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, performance and SDK status, used for troubleshooting and fraud prevention.

If you decline tracking, the SDK does not receive the IDFA; the Game still works normally and ads are served as non-personalized ads.

2. How the information is used

  • To serve ads (personalized if you consent, otherwise non-personalized);
  • To measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud;
  • To diagnose crashes and performance issues and keep the Game stable.

3. Third-party services

The Game uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (ad serving): Google Privacy Policy, AdMob data disclosure;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent management where required by law);
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (system tracking permission, iOS only).

4. Your choices

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — you can turn tracking off for the Game at any time;
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising — turn off personalized ads and reset the advertising identifier;
  • Uninstalling the Game stops further collection by the Game (data already collected by third parties remains subject to their policies).

5. Data retention

The Game does not store the ad data described above itself; retention by third-party SDKs follows their privacy policies. Game saves stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SDKs or regulations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact: gxhdky2345@gmail.com.
